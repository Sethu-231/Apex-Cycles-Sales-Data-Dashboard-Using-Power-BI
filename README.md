# 🚴 Apex Cycles – Power BI Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

**Apex Cycles** is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze sales performance, revenue, profit, orders, customers, products, and regional performance for a cycling business.

The dashboard transforms business data into interactive visual insights to help understand sales trends, product performance, customer behavior, and geographic distribution.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze overall revenue and profit performance
* Track total orders and bike return rate
* Identify high-performing product categories and subcategories
* Analyze monthly revenue trends
* Compare regional sales performance
* Understand customer segments and occupations
* Explore customer and product-level details
* Present business insights through an interactive dashboard

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Power BI**
* **DAX**
* **Power Query**
* **Data Modeling**
* **Data Visualization**

## 📌 Dashboard Features

### Exclusive Dashboard

* Total Revenue
* Total Profit
* Total Orders
* Bike Return Rate
* Monthly Revenue Trend
* Revenue by Product Category
* Revenue by Product Subcategory
* Regional Revenue Analysis

### Customer Detail

* Customer-level analysis
* Orders by occupation
* Income-level filtering
* Top customer order analysis

### Product Detail

* Product-level performance analysis
* Product filtering and comparison

### Map

* Geographic revenue analysis
* Regional performance comparison

## 📈 Key Insights

The dashboard provides an interactive view of business performance, allowing users to identify revenue trends, profitable product segments, regional performance, and customer behavior.
